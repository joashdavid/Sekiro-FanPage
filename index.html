01 — THE STORY

A WOLF BOUND
BY BLOOD & HONOR

戦国の末

In the twilight of a shattered Sengoku Japan, a nameless shinobi is bound by an oath — and by the Dragon's Blood that will not let him die. His young lord is taken. His arm is severed. His name, forgotten.

They call him the One-Armed Wolf. And the Wolf does not hesitate.

Carve a path through Ashina's war-torn lands. Study every blade that seeks your neck. Deflect, counter, and die — as many times as it takes. For death is not the end of the Wolf's tale. It is the beginning.
